## Behavioral explanation
Bidirectional dbcman **data transfer**, RPC **fno 0x80001318**. Marshals two context words (`param_1`,`param_2`), an **input length** (`*param_3`) and an **auxiliary count** (`*param_5`), plus an inline **input payload** (`*param_3` bytes copied from `param_4` into buffer `+0x10`). After the call it reads a signed result at `+0x410`; on success it returns the updated length/aux via the same out-params and copies **`out_count` bytes** of reply payload from buffer `+0x210` into `param_6`. This is the dbcman **send-and-receive** primitive (companion to `dbcman_transact` fno 0x319 and `dbcman_send_data2` fno 0x31b).
