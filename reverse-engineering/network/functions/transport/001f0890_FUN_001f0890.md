## Behavioral explanation
SN@P transport RPC stub for opcode **0x2d = room TRANSFER** (named in the brief). This is the largest
reply in the batch (`rsize=0x128`): it fetches a ~0x100-byte data block plus two trailing u32 words from
the IOP room state. It sends a header-only request, then unpacks the reply: `FUN_00109eb8` copies the
reply blob at `B+0x1c` into the caller's `param_1` buffer, and the two u32 at `B+0x11c` / `B+0x120` are
written back through `param_2` / `param_3`. "Transfer" = pull a room/member data snapshot from the IOP to
the EE.

## Input / output
- **param_1** (void*): destination for the reply data block (copied from `B+0x1c`; ~0x100 bytes — the
  span from `+0x1c` up to the two trailing words at `+0x11c`).
- **param_2** (u32*): receives the first trailing word (`B+0x11c`, `uRam0037c01c`).
- **param_3** (u32*): receives the second trailing word (`B+0x120`, `uRam0037c020`).
- **Returns** (u16): IOP status word from `B+0x18`.

## Side effects

- Writes `*param_2`, `*param_3`, and `param_1[0..~0x100]`.
