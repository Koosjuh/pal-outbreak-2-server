`param_1`), a 1-byte tag (`param_2`), a zeroed 2-byte status word, and a zeroed byte at payload+0xf.

(`cRam003435b5 == 2`, e.g. shutting down), or when the queue is full (`count >= 8`). On the first
successful enqueue after idle it raises the "kick" flag `cRam0029f2f0` so the consumer starts
