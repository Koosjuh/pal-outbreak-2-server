`MWSFD_PS2EE_Ver_2_88_Build_Jul_2_2002`). `param_1` is a config/descriptor object. On the **first**
entry (`DAT_0022d9d0 == 0`) it runs the full one-time bring-up: publishes the version-string
pointer, initialises the sub-libraries (the FUN_00128468/0013da78/0013d540/0013e2a0 chain),

`param_1[0]` (a seconds value: `param_1[0] * 1000 * 0.5`, rounded) and installs it, registers a
periodic worker/callback (FUN_0013b750(0x15c908,0)), and finishes wiring the descriptor. Every entry

refcount. A null `param_1` is rejected with a log message.
