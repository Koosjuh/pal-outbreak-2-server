## Behavioral explanation
Resolves a TLV record by the caller-supplied key (`FUN_001999f0(param_1, 0)`) and returns its count
word (`+0x04`), or `0` if no such record exists. This is the keyed counterpart of `FUN_00199850`
(which counts the *current* record with the high-bit adjustment); here there is **no** high-bit
adjustment and a proper NULL-guard, so it is the plain "how many entries does record `key` hold"
accessor.

- **Return:** `undefined4` — `record->count` (`*(u32*)(rec+4)`), or `0` when the record is not found.
