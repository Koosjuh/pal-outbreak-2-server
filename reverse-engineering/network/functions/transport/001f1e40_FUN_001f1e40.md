## Behavioral explanation
Resets an EE-side **local SN@P bookkeeping table** to empty under a dedicated lock. Takes the
0x0f60/0x0f70 lock pair (a mutex distinct from the IPC-call mutex 0x1ef3e0/0x1ef3f0, guarding the
0x37df50 region that sits alongside the SN@P IPC queue 0x37df10 and buffers), zeroes 0x9a0 bytes at
0x37df50, clears the count/valid word at 0x37df48, then unlocks. This is the "clear the cached
roster/list" operation invoked on session start or teardown so stale entries don't leak across
sessions.
